1. Define the "Reference Trace"
Create a baseline sequence representing the Model Curriculum (the "Standard Path").
Standard Path: [1,2,3,4]

Student Path: [1,2,_,3]
 (indicating a fail and a gap).
2. Measure "Distance" from the Norm
Use Edit Distance (Levenshtein Distance) or Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) to calculate a "Turbulence Score."
Low Score: The student follows the map perfectly.
High Score: The student has many "insertions" (retakes), "deletions" (skipped semesters), or "substitutions" (taking electives out of order).

3. Identify "Branching Points"
Analyze at which specific $subject$ students typically deviate.

Bottleneck Detection: If 60% of students deviate after "Organic Chemistry," that subject is a structural divergence point.
